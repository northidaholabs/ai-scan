The scan answers

You say the job once. Everything after that follows from it.

  1. The capture is planned to answer that question, worked out from the scan in front of it.
  2. Each answer carries what it is worth.
  3. What could not be answered is declined, with the reason, rather than guessed.
  4. Nothing is left open, and the person signs once.

The four answers below come from a demonstration scan of one building. Each one says what it is worth, and how much of the building it could not answer.

What it won’t answer

So what did you not get?

On this building the scan answered the plumb question on 5 of the 33 walls. The other 28 are in the report with their picture and their coverage, and no plumb figure.

The rule is written into the report: a wall less than 80% covered, shorter than 4 m, or rougher than 15 mm RMS against one plane gives the reason instead of a number. Every wall that did answer had been seen along at least 82% of its length. Across all 33, coverage runs from 29% to 100%.

The refusals sit in the report beside the answers rather than being left out of it. That is the part you can act on: it tells you which wall to go back for.
