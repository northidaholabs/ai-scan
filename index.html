How it works

  1. On site: you carry the scanner and say yes once. A foundation model, through MCP, looks at the station and marks the next setup and the landmarks.
  2. After every station: the software places it with the others, checks the placement with independent tests, and redraws the site map.
  3. Afterwards: the registered scans become reports: wall heat maps, ground and water maps, sections, CSV and DXF.
